Common questions

How much does an AI video ad cost?+

A fraction of a traditional shoot, because there's no crew, no locations, and no reshoots. Your price depends on video length and how many versions you need, and you get a fixed quote before we start.

Is it legal to use AI-generated videos for advertising?+

Yes. Your video is made on commercial-use plans, and you get full rights to use it in paid ads, social media, and on your website. I also keep every video free of real people's faces, famous logos, and protected characters, so it's clean from day one.

Will an AI video ad improve my ROI?+

Yes, through two things: lower production cost and faster testing. You can launch several versions of the same ad, see which one performs, and put your media budget behind the winner, without paying for a new shoot each time.

Can I run AI-generated ads on YouTube, Meta, and TikTok?+

Yes. AI-generated ads run on all three platforms, and each one handles its own AI labeling.

How do I choose the right AI video partner?+

Choose someone who can show you work close to your industry, and who owns the whole process. I handle everything myself, from script to visuals, editing, and sound, so your brand voice stays consistent from the first frame to the last, and you deal with one person who knows your project.

Can you make testimonial or UGC-style ads with AI?+

No. A testimonial only works when it comes from a real customer, so I don't make fake ones, and I'd steer you away from anyone who offers to. What I do make well: product and promo videos, brand films, and explainers.
